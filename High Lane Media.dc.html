Our story

High Lane started in a spare room with two laptops and one client's Instagram account. The content worked, the enquiries came in — and then the same question kept arriving: “can you handle the replies too?”, “can you fix the website they land on?”

So we learned to. Automations came first, out of necessity. Web development followed, because a great campaign pointing at a broken site is just expensive noise. We are still small on purpose: you talk to the people doing the work.
